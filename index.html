Privacy Policy for Box2Pallet – Stack Pattern Tool

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Zomplant Apps provides Box2Pallet, a tool for calculating and displaying box arrangements on pallets.

Planning data and local storage

Box2Pallet calculates box arrangements on your device. Zomplant Apps does not receive your dimensions, calculated patterns, or saved library from these calculations or local storage. The app does not require an app account and does not include advertising or analytics services.

Depending on the app version, local data can include box and pallet dimensions, overhang, measurement units, color theme, pattern options, and saved patterns with names, box positions, settings, and lock status. Manually created patterns are stored in the same local library. The app does not provide an account-based pattern synchronization service.

Printing and PDF copies

When you choose Print pattern, the selected pattern sheet is passed to your device's printing services. It includes the arrangement, dimensions, box count, overhang, and pattern information shown on the sheet. The printer, print service, or PDF destination you choose may process or keep a copy under its own policies. Zomplant Apps does not receive these print jobs from the app.

If you save, share, or print a copy, you control where it goes and who receives it. Copies of product or job information and notes that you add to a PDF or printed sheet are handled by you and any services or people you choose.

Recovery, device backups, and deletion

To support deletion Undo and file recovery, the app can retain recently deleted patterns and previous library data in local recovery files. Prior data may remain in these files until replaced. Clearing the app's storage using your device settings removes the app's local preferences, library, and recovery files.

Your operating system may include app settings and saved patterns in device backups or device transfers according to your platform and backup settings. These services are controlled by your device and platform provider, not Zomplant Apps. Restoring a backup can restore earlier app data. Uninstalling or clearing app storage does not necessarily delete platform backups, printed sheets, or PDF copies you saved elsewhere; manage those separately through their location or service.

Device access

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, photos, microphone, or camera for pallet planning. Printing services and external services operate under their own permissions and policies.

External services and support

If you open a link, such as this privacy-policy page, your browser and the website provider handle that visit under their own policies. Google Play and your device's operating system also handle their services under their own policies.

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information about visits to this website, including IP addresses, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. This webpage does not add analytics, advertisements, or tracking scripts.

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use that information to respond to your request. Do not include sensitive information in support messages. Contact us if you want us to delete a support message, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Children's privacy

Box2Pallet is a general-purpose pallet planning tool. Zomplant Apps does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app's practices change. We will post the revised policy here and update the date above.
